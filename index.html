Clip Factory

A private tool used by the Peak ClipsHQ channel.

Clip Factory is a desktop program that runs on one personal computer. It turns gameplay recordings that the channel owner has permission to use into short vertical clips.

When the owner connects their TikTok account, Clip Factory can send a finished clip to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is posted publicly by Clip Factory: the owner opens the draft in the TikTok app, writes the caption and decides whether to post it.

Clip Factory is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.
