1. What Clip Factory is

Clip Factory is a private desktop tool used by the owner of the Peak ClipsHQ channel to make short clips and send them to the owner's own TikTok drafts and YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

2. Who may use it

Only the channel owner, on their own computer, with their own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

3. Content

Only footage the owner has the necessary rights or permission to use is processed. The owner is responsible for what they choose to post, and for following TikTok's and YouTube's terms and community guidelines.

4. TikTok

Clip Factory only uploads videos to the connected account's TikTok inbox as drafts, after the owner has connected the account. The owner reviews, edits and posts each draft in the TikTok app. The owner can disconnect Clip Factory at any time in Clip Factory or in TikTok's settings.

Clip Factory is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
