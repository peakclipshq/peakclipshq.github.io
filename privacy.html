Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who this covers

Clip Factory is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Peak ClipsHQ channel. It has no other users.

What data it uses

  • TikTok: when the owner connects their TikTok account, Clip Factory receives an access token and basic account information (such as the account's display name), and uses the video.upload permission to send the owner's own clips to their TikTok drafts.
  • YouTube: an access token for uploading the owner's clips to their own channel and reading those videos' view and like counts.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. Clip Factory has no servers, does not sell or share any data, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect information about anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect TikTok or YouTube in Clip Factory at any time, which deletes the stored tokens, and can also remove access in TikTok's or Google's account settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the Peak ClipsHQ channel through its YouTube "About" page.
